Connect Tripadvisor to bring your Tripadvisor reviews into the Feedelity inbox.

## Add your Tripadvisor page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Tripadvisor">
    Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**, click **Add integration**, and choose **Tripadvisor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Open your page on Tripadvisor in your browser. Copy the link from the address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the link">
    On **Add your listings**, paste the link into **Page link** and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your location">
    If the page has no location yet, pick one on **Link your locations** and click **Finish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add another page later, open your Tripadvisor integration and click **Add channel**.

## Reply to Tripadvisor reviews

Feedelity can't post replies on Tripadvisor for you. You write or generate the reply in Feedelity, then post it on Tripadvisor yourself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your reply">
    Open the case and write or generate your reply. A reply can be up to 2,000 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    Click **Send**. Feedelity copies your reply and opens the review on Tripadvisor in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post it on Tripadvisor">
    Paste the reply on Tripadvisor and post it. Then close the case in Feedelity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Replies can take up to 48 hours to appear due to Tripadvisor's processing policies.
</Note>
