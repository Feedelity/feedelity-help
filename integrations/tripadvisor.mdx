---
title: "Tripadvisor"
description: "Connect your Tripadvisor location by pasting its URL into Feedelity."
---

To connect Tripadvisor, copy the URL of your Tripadvisor location and paste it into Feedelity. Once connected, reviews appear in Feedelity automatically.

![Pasting a Tripadvisor URL into Feedelity](/images/integrations/tripadvisor/1.png)

Example URLs:

```text
www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review...
www.tripadvisor.be/Hotel_Review...
www.tripadvisor.co.uk/AttractionProductReview...
```

<Note>
  You can't reply to Tripadvisor reviews directly from Feedelity. Instead, generate a response in Feedelity and click **Copy & Send**. This copies the suggested reply and opens the right page on Tripadvisor in one click, where you paste and post your response.
</Note>
