A voucher is one guest's copy of an [offer](/loyalty/offers). Each voucher has a unique code that the guest shows to redeem it with you.

<Note>
  Loyalty (games, offers, and vouchers) is included in the trial and the Standard plan. On the Enterprise plan, it's an add-on. If your plan doesn't include it, **Games**, **Offers**, and **Vouchers** are grayed out in **Settings** with a lock and an **Add-on** label. Contact your account manager to add it.
</Note>

## How guests get vouchers

When a guest wins a prize in one of your [games](/loyalty/games), Feedelity issues a voucher for that offer:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Voucher is created">
    Feedelity generates a unique code, or takes the next available code from the offer's code pool. The code is marked as issued, so it's never reused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voucher is sent">
    The guest receives the voucher by email, with the offer details and their unique code. You can [customize this email](/loyalty/offers#customize-the-voucher-email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voucher is tracked">
    Every voucher and its code is listed on the **Vouchers** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Delivery platform credit is different. When you [reply to a Deliveroo or Uber Eats review](/inbox/credit), you can add credit that's applied to the guest's next order on that platform. That credit isn't an offer, and it doesn't appear on the **Vouchers** page.
</Note>

## View vouchers

Go to **Settings** > **Vouchers**, in the **Loyalty** section. The list shows each voucher's **Code**, **Offer**, **Customer** email, and when it was **Issued**.

- Search by email address or code.
- Filter by offer or by date range.

Click a voucher to see its details and the exact **Email** the guest received. From there, click **View offer** to open the offer.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/vouchers — the vouchers list with the offer filter and search */}

Use the code to look up which guest received which voucher, for example when a guest redeems it at the counter.

<Note>
  Feedelity doesn't track redemptions or expiry dates. Record redemptions in your POS system.
</Note>
