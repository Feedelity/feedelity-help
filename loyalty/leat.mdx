If you run your loyalty program in Leat, you can connect it to Feedelity. Your Leat promotions then appear as offers in Feedelity. Use them as prizes in your [games](/loyalty/games). Guests who win get a real Leat voucher.

<Note>
  Leat is available on request. Contact your account manager to turn it on for your organization.
</Note>

## Connect Leat

Connect your Leat account first. You need a personal access token from your Leat dashboard. See [Leat](/integrations/leat) for the steps.

When you connect, Feedelity imports all your Leat promotions right away. After that, Feedelity checks Leat for new and changed promotions every 15 minutes.

## Create a promotion in Leat

Every prize from Leat starts as a promotion in your Leat dashboard. A promotion sets the rules for its vouchers, such as when they're valid and how often they can be used.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open promotions in Leat">
    In your [Leat dashboard](https://business.leat.com), go to **Loyalty** > **Promotions & Vouchers** ([open in Leat](https://business.leat.com/loyalty/promotions)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the promotion">
    Click **New promotion**. Enter a name and a description and, if needed, how many times each voucher code can be used. Click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the validity">
    On the **Validity** tab, choose when vouchers become active and when they expire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the limitations">
    On the **Limitations** tab, choose how many voucher codes the promotion allows in total, and how many each contact can redeem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in Feedelity">
    Within 15 minutes, the promotion appears under **Settings** > **Offers**. Add it as a prize to a [game](/loyalty/games).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep these points in mind:

- **Write the name and description for guests.** Feedelity uses them as the name and description of the offer, and guests see them in their voucher email. Include the conditions, such as a minimum spend and how long the voucher is valid. Feedelity doesn't show the expiry date that Leat sets.
- **Don't generate vouchers in Leat.** Feedelity asks Leat for a new voucher each time a guest wins, so you don't need **Add vouchers** in the promotion.
- **Leave room in the limitations.** When a promotion reaches its total number of voucher codes, or a guest reaches the number of codes per contact, Leat refuses the voucher and the guest who won doesn't get one. Guests can play a game again every 24 hours with the same email address.

For more about promotions in Leat, see the Leat article [How to create a voucher](https://support.leat.com/en/articles/9375160-how-to-create-a-voucher).

## Your promotions in Feedelity

Your Leat promotions are listed under **Settings** > **Offers**, with the Leat icon and **Leat promotion** under the name. They show the same **Active** or **Inactive** status as in Leat.

Leat is where you manage these promotions. The offer page shows: "This offer is managed in Leat. Any changes you make to this offer in Leat show up in Feedelity automatically."

- **Name, description, and status**: change them in your Leat dashboard. Feedelity has no **Edit offer**, **Deactivate offer**, or **Delete offer** option for Leat promotions.
- **Codes**: Leat creates the codes, so the offer page has no **Voucher codes** section.
- **Voucher email**: you can customize the email in Feedelity, the same way as for Feedelity offers. See [Customize the voucher email](/loyalty/offers#customize-the-voucher-email).

When a promotion is inactive in Leat, the offer page warns you that customers can no longer receive vouchers for this offer. Activate the promotion in your Leat dashboard to make vouchers available again. Inactive promotions can't be added as a prize, and games skip them.

## How guests get a Leat voucher

When a guest wins a Leat promotion in one of your games:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The guest is added to Leat">
    Feedelity looks up the guest in Leat by email address. If the guest isn't a contact in Leat yet, Feedelity adds them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leat creates the voucher">
    Leat creates a voucher for the promotion, with its own code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Feedelity sends the voucher">
    The guest receives the voucher email from Feedelity, with the Leat code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The voucher is listed under **Settings** > **Vouchers**, like every other voucher, and in your Leat dashboard.
