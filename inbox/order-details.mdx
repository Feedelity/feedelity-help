---
title: "Order details"
description: "See what the customer ordered on Uber Eats and Deliveroo, right under their review."
---

Reviews from [Uber Eats](/integrations/uber-eats) and [Deliveroo](/integrations/deliveroo) show the order the customer placed, under the review:

- The order number, such as **Order #1832**. Click it to open the order in Uber Eats Manager or Deliveroo Partner Hub.
- When the order was placed, and its total.
- Each item with its quantity and price, and the options the customer chose underneath.

Use it to check what the customer ordered before you [reply](/inbox/replying), for example when they report a missing item. To make it up to them, [add credit to your reply](/inbox/credit).

## When the order doesn't show

- Deliveroo only shares the order for reviews with a written comment. Reviews without one show **Order details are not available for reviews without a written comment.**
- If the platform no longer has the order, the review shows without it.
- If the order can't be loaded right now, you see **Order details could not be loaded.**, and the review shows without it.
