## What is it?

Guests scan the QR code, leave a review, and spin the wheel. If they win a reward, they receive a voucher in their email inbox. [Watch this video](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/KpMaPBa3U5M) to see what this looks like for your guests.

Go to **Settings** > **Games**, in the **Loyalty** section, to create your own game in a few minutes.

We recommend printing the QR codes on flyers, for example in takeaway or delivery bags, and on plaques on the tables. Design a visual that includes:

- A photo of a wheel of fortune
- The QR code
- Your restaurant logo
- An inviting message, such as "Love your experience? Let us know and spin to win!"
- A brief explanation: "1. Scan the QR code 2. Leave a 5-star review 3. Come back to spin the wheel and win a reward"
- All text in your brand colors, both on the visual and in Feedelity. You can customize the colors, logo, background, and more in Feedelity.
- Make it fun!

## Why is this interesting?

Guests often only feel the need to leave a review when they're dissatisfied or angry. A satisfied guest is less likely to pick up their phone and give five stars. The wheel of fortune gives that silent majority a fun incentive to do just that.

The result:

- Happy guests, because they win a reward
- A fun activity your guests remember
- 12 times more 5-star reviews
- More returning customers
- Extra turnover from vouchers with a minimum spend

## How do the vouchers work?

Good rewards are the cheaper items on your menu, such as a drink, a small dessert, or ice cream. You can also offer a discount, for example €2 on a minimum spend of €20.

We recommend making the voucher valid for 1 month, starting the day after the guest wins the reward. This way, guests have to return to redeem it. About 1 in 20 guests redeem their vouchers.

When a guest spins the wheel and wins one of your rewards, they receive a voucher by email. A voucher is a unique code or QR code.

To see how many vouchers were redeemed and by whom, go to **Settings** > **Vouchers**, in the **Loyalty** section.

<Warning>
  Before you launch the wheel of fortune, add the rewards to your POS system. This way, you and your staff can record everything properly.
</Warning>

## Launch checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your wheel of fortune">
    Go to **Settings** > **Games** to create and personalize the wheel of fortune. Decide on your rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your POS and team">
    Add the rewards as buttons in your POS system. Tell your team how guests redeem rewards and how staff must log them in the POS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print flyers or plaques">
    Make fun flyers or plaques with the wheel of fortune QR code on them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it! Enjoy the five-star reviews and recurring revenue.

## Is the wheel of fortune allowed under Google's policy?

Yes. Google's policy prohibits offering rewards *in exchange for a review*, but the wheel of fortune works differently. The reward is tied to **spinning the wheel**, not to leaving a review or to the number of stars. Every guest who spins has a chance at the same prize, whether they leave a review or not. The reward and the review are fully separate, so it stays within Google's rules.
