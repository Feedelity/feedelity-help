In Feedelity, your account belongs to you and your organization belongs to your company. One account can belong to several organizations, with a different role in each.

## Accounts, organizations, and roles

- **Account**: you, identified by your email address. You use the same account and password for every organization. See [Log in](/getting-started/log-in).
- **Organization**: your company's space in Feedelity, usually one restaurant brand. It holds the locations, channels, feedback, users, and settings. Billing is per organization.
- **Role**: what you can do in one organization. You have exactly one role in each organization you belong to.
- **Location access**: which locations you see in that organization. Your access can be all locations or only some.

For example, you can be an **Administrator** in your own brand's organization and a **Viewer** in a franchise partner's organization. Each organization only sees its own data.

## Roles

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Full access to all locations and settings. Can invite users, manage integrations and billing, and send credit and vouchers. |
| **Member** | View, edit, and reply to feedback for assigned locations. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to feedback and reports for assigned locations. Can still add comments to a case. |

See [Users](/settings/users#user-roles) for the full comparison.

## Location access

Administrators always see all locations. For members and viewers, an administrator chooses **All locations** or **Specific locations**. With specific locations, you see the locations and [location groups](/settings/location-groups) you're assigned to, and the inbox, dashboards, and notifications only show feedback from those locations.

See [Location access](/settings/users#location-access).

### Accept an invitation

An administrator [invites you](/settings/users#invite-users) from **Settings** > **Users** and chooses your role and locations. You get an email with a **Join** button.

1. Click the button in the email. If you don't have an account yet, [create one](/getting-started/create-account) with the email address the invitation was sent to.
2. Log in. Feedelity shows the invitation.
3. Click **Accept**, or **Reject** to decline.

Already in another organization? Your invitations also appear in the sidebar. Click your organization name at the top of the sidebar and find the invitation under **Pending Invitations**. Click ✓ to accept or ✕ to reject.

### Request access

When an organization has registered your company's email domain, for example `yourcompany.com`, Feedelity shows it to you after you sign up with an address on that domain.

1. Optionally, add a message to the administrator.
2. Click **Request access**.

An administrator of that organization approves or rejects your request and chooses your role and locations. We email you once they decide. See [Join requests](/settings/users#join-requests).

<Note>
  An organization's email domain is registered automatically when someone creates the organization with a company email address. Personal addresses, such as Gmail or Outlook.com, are never registered.
</Note>

## What happens when you log in

After you log in, Feedelity checks, in this order:

1. **You belong to an organization**: Feedelity opens it. If you belong to several, it opens the first one alphabetically.
2. **You have an invitation**: Feedelity asks you to accept or reject it.
3. **You requested access**: Feedelity shows that your request is waiting for approval.
4. **Your email domain is registered**: Feedelity offers to request access.
5. **None of these**: Feedelity asks you to [create an organization](/getting-started/create-organization).

## Switch organization

1. Click your organization name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Click **Switch organization**.
3. Pick an organization. The checkmark shows the one you're in.

**Switch organization** only appears when you belong to two or more organizations. With more than eight, you can search the list.

## Create another organization

To set up an organization for another business, go to [app.feedelity.io/organizations/new](https://app.feedelity.io/organizations/new) while you're logged in. You follow the same steps as in [Create your organization](/getting-started/create-organization) and become the new organization's administrator. Each organization has its own trial and license.

## Leave an organization

You can leave an organization from your profile. You lose access to all of that organization's data, and an administrator has to invite you again to get back in. See [Leave an organization](/profile/profile#leave-an-organization).

To remove someone else, an administrator uses [Remove user](/settings/users#manage-a-user).
