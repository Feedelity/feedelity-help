This guide takes you from a new account to your first reply. Setup takes a few minutes.

## Before you begin

You need:

- Access to the Google account that manages your Google Business Profile
- A work email address, or a Microsoft account

Every new organization starts with a 2-week free trial. You don't need a credit card.

## Set up your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.feedelity.io/signup](https://app.feedelity.io/signup). Enter your email, or click **Continue with Microsoft**.

    If you sign up with email, enter the code we send you. Then add your name and choose a password with at least 12 characters.

    <Note>
      If a colleague already uses Feedelity, you may see an invitation to join their organization instead. Click **Accept** to join.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Answer a few questions about your business:

    - Your brand name, the name customers know you by
    - What kind of business you run
    - How many locations you operate
    - Your phone number

    Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google Business Profile">
    Click **Connect Google Business Profile**, then **Continue to Google**.

    Sign in with the Google account that manages your locations. On Google's consent screen, tick the box that gives Feedelity access to your reviews.

    Feedelity imports reviews from all your locations.

    <Tip>
      Not ready yet? Click **Not now, connect later in Settings**. You can connect Google anytime under **Settings** > **Integrations**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply to your first review">
    Open **Inbox** and click a case. Click **Generate** to let your AI reply agent draft a reply.

    Edit the draft, or click **Improve** to adjust tone, length, or language. Click **Send** when you're happy. The case moves to the **Closed** folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Next steps

The **Set up your workspace** checklist in the app tracks the rest of your setup.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Add more channels" icon="plug">
    Connect Tripadvisor, delivery apps, social media, and email under **Settings** > **Integrations**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invite your team" icon="users">
    Add colleagues under **Settings** > **Users** to handle feedback together.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tune your AI reply agent" icon="bot">
    Describe how your brand communicates under **Settings** > **Agents**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automate simple replies" icon="zap">
    Create a template under **Settings** > **Templates** to answer star-only reviews automatically.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
