**AI chat** lets you ask questions about your feedback in plain language. It answers from your own data: your ratings, cases, tags, and customer messages.

It also answers questions about how to use Feedelity. Those answers come from this help center and link to the article they're based on.

Open it from the top of the sidebar, under **Home**.

<Note>AI chat is experimental, and answers may contain mistakes. Check important numbers in your [dashboards](/analytics/dashboard).</Note>

## Availability

AI chat is part of Feedelity Pro. The icon next to **AI chat** in the sidebar shows your access:

- **Diamond icon**: you have access as part of Feedelity Pro. Hover over the icon to see the details.
- **Lock icon**: AI chat is an add-on that your organization doesn't have yet. The item is greyed out.

## What you can ask

AI chat can look up:

- **Numbers**: the number of cases, average star rating, NPS, response rate, red flags, and how many customers gave each star rating
- **Topics**: how often customers mention a tag and whether they're positive, neutral, or negative about it
- **Comparisons**: split by location, channel, tag, or period, and compare with the previous period or the same period last year
- **Example messages**: what customers actually wrote, or how your team replied
- **How to use Feedelity**: how to set something up or where to find it in the app. The answer ends with a link to the help center article, which opens in a new tab.

You can filter on locations, channels, tags, star ratings, red flags, whether a case got a reply, and language.

## Example questions

- How are we doing this month?
- Which location has the lowest rating in the last 30 days?
- What do customers complain about most on Uber Eats?
- How did our response rate change compared to last quarter?
- What do customers say about our service in Antwerp?
- Show me some recent 1-star reviews about delivery.
- How do I connect Uber Eats?
- How do I invite a colleague?
- How do I set up automatic replies for star-only reviews?

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Type your question in the box. You can ask in your own language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press **Enter** or click the arrow. Use **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    AI chat looks up your data and writes an answer. Ask a follow-up question in the same conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop an answer while it's being written, click the stop button or press **Esc**.

## See where an answer comes from

While AI chat looks up data, you see what it's checking, such as "Comparing average scores per location for last month". When it's done, click **Used … tools** to see each lookup. Click a lookup to see its **Input** and **Result**.

## Rate an answer

Below each answer you can:

- **Copy** the answer
- Click the thumbs up or thumbs down to rate it, and tell us what you liked or what went wrong

Your ratings help us improve AI chat.

## Limits

- AI chat answers from your Feedelity data and this help center. It can't answer questions about data that isn't in Feedelity, such as revenue or staffing.
- It only sees the locations you have access to.
- Unless you name a period, it uses all your feedback. The answer says which period it used.
- When a number is based on only a few reviews, the answer says so.
- For a prioritized list of what to fix, see [Tasks](/issues-and-tasks/tasks).
- Conversations aren't saved. When you leave or refresh the page, the conversation is gone.
