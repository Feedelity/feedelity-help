title: "Integration disconnected or no new reviews"
description: "What to do when feedback stops arriving."
---

If new reviews or messages stop showing up in your inbox, check these causes in order.

## The integration needs to be reconnected

Sometimes a platform ends Feedelity's access to your account. Until you reconnect, new feedback stops arriving and replies may fail.

Administrators see a warning at the top of the **Inbox**: **Your *platform* account *name* needs your attention**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /inbox — the reconnect warning above the case list with the Reconnect now button */}

To fix it:

1. Click **Reconnect now** in the warning.
2. Log in to the platform with the account that manages your locations, and allow access.

You can also reconnect from **Settings** > **Integrations**. Accounts that need it show **Reauthentication required** in the **Status** column. Click **Reconnect**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/integrations — an account with the Reauthentication required status and the Reconnect button */}

<Note>
  Only administrators see this warning. If you're a member or viewer, ask an administrator to reconnect.
</Note>

## Facebook and Instagram are temporarily unavailable

If you see the warning **Facebook and Instagram are temporarily unavailable**, the problem is on our side. New Facebook and Instagram messages don't come in for now, and you can't reply to them. You don't need to do anything: we're working on a fix. Click **Dismiss** to hide the warning.

## The location is closed or removed on the platform

Open **Settings** > **Integrations** and click the account. Check the status of each channel under **Channels**:

- **Inactive**: the location is temporarily or permanently closed on the platform. Feedelity can't fetch new data while it stays closed.
- **Deleted**: the channel was deleted from your account on the platform. Feedelity can't fetch new data for it.

To fix it, reopen the location on the platform, or add the channel again if it moved to a new listing. See [Manage integrations](/integrations/manage).

## The feedback arrives but you can't find it

Feedback may be arriving without showing where you expect it:

- **The channel isn't linked to a location.** Its feedback isn't assigned to a location, so it disappears when you filter by location. In **Settings** > **Integrations**, open the account and link each channel to a location under **Channels**.
- **Inbox automation moves it to Closed.** Each integration can send new messages straight to the **Closed** folder. Check **Inbox automation** on the integration, and look under **Closed** or **All** in the inbox.
- **A filter is on.** Clear your inbox filters. See [Filter and search](/inbox/filtering-and-searching).

## There are simply no new reviews

Feedelity fetches feedback from each platform on a schedule, so new reviews can take a while to appear. If a quiet location stays empty, check the platform itself for new reviews.
