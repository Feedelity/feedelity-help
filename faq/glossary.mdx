---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The terms used in Feedelity."
---

## AI chat

A chat where you ask questions about your feedback in plain language. It answers from your own data, and also answers questions about how to use Feedelity. See [AI chat](/ai-chat).

## AI reply agent

An AI assistant that drafts replies to your customers in your brand's tone. You set it up under **Settings** > **Agents**. See [AI reply agents](/automation/agents).

## API key

The key your own systems use to pull feedback data from Feedelity through the Data Export API. API access is part of the Enterprise plan. See [API keys](/api/api-keys).

## Auto reply

Automatic replies to reviews without text, so no star rating goes unanswered. Rules decide which reviews get a reply and what it says. See [Auto reply](/automation/auto-reply).

## Bulk send

A page where you reply to many open reviews one after the other, 20 at a time. See [Bulk send](/inbox/bulk-actions).

## Case

One piece of feedback in your inbox, such as a review, a message, or an email. A case holds the customer's message and all replies to it. See [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

## Channel

A feedback source for one location, such as a Google Business profile or an Uber Eats store. You connect channels through an integration and link each one to a location. See [How integrations work](/integrations/how-integrations-work).

## Competitor

A nearby business on Google that you compare one of your locations with, on rating, review volume, and response speed. See [Competitors](/analytics/competitors).

## Credit

An amount you add to a reply to a Deliveroo or Uber Eats review. The customer gets it on their next order on that platform. Credit isn't an offer or a voucher. See [Add credit to a reply](/inbox/credit).

## Dashboard

A visual summary of your guest feedback, with your key metrics over time and what guests talk about. Open it from **Reporting** > **Dashboard**. See [Dashboard](/analytics/dashboard).

## Folder

Your own category for cases, such as complaints or catering requests. Each case can be in one folder. See [Folders](/inbox/folders).

## Form

Questions you ask your guests, from a quick feedback survey to a contact form. You share a form with a link, a QR code, or on your website. See [Forms](/collecting-feedback/forms).

## Game

A spin-to-win game guests play after they leave a Google review. Guests who win receive an offer as a voucher by email. See [Games](/loyalty/games).

## Integration

The connection between Feedelity and an account on a platform, such as Google or Uber Eats. Most integrations bring in channels with feedback, and one integration can bring in many channels. A loyalty integration such as Leat brings in promotions instead. See [Manage integrations](/integrations/manage).

## Issue

A specific problem a customer ran into that you can fix, such as "Food served cold". Feedelity finds issues in your feedback automatically. See [Issues](/issues-and-tasks/issues).

## Leat promotion

A promotion from your Leat loyalty program that appears in Feedelity as an offer. You manage it in Leat, and guests who win it get a Leat voucher. See [Leat promotions](/loyalty/leat).

## Location

A single restaurant. Each case belongs to a location, so you can filter the inbox and dashboards by restaurant. See [Locations](/settings/locations).

## Location group

A set of locations you group together, for example by region or brand. Use it to filter several locations at once. See [Location groups](/settings/location-groups).

## Message

One entry in a case. The customer's review or email is a message, and so is each reply your team sends.

## Notification

A message that tells you something needs your attention, such as an urgent case. You see notifications under the bell icon and get them by email. See [Notifications](/profile/notifications).

## Offer

A reward you set up once, like a free coffee. Each guest who receives it gets a voucher. An offer is either a Feedelity offer or a promotion from a loyalty platform such as Leat. See [Offers](/loyalty/offers).

## Opinion

One statement about one topic inside a piece of feedback. "The burger was great, but the fries were cold" holds two opinions. Each opinion has its own sentiment. Also called a claim. See [Opinions](/analytics/opinions).

## Order details

The order a customer placed, shown under their Uber Eats or Deliveroo review: the items, the total, and when the order was placed. See [Order details](/inbox/order-details).

## Organization

Your company's account in Feedelity, usually one restaurant brand. An organization holds your locations, users, and settings. You can belong to more than one organization. See [How users and organizations work](/getting-started/users-and-organizations).

## Priority

Marks a case as **Urgent** when it needs fast attention. Urgent cases stand out in the case list, and your teammates with access to the location get a notification. See [Tags, sentiment, and priority](/inbox/tags-sentiment-priority#priority).

## QR code

A code guests scan in your restaurant to give feedback. Happy guests go on to leave a Google review, and unhappy guests reach you privately in your inbox. See [QR codes](/collecting-feedback/qr-codes).

## Red flag

An urgent issue in a piece of feedback, such as food poisoning. Feedelity alerts the teammates who have access to that location. See [Red flags](/settings/flags).

## Role

What a user can do in an organization. An **Administrator** has full access to all locations and settings. A **Member** can view, edit, and reply to feedback for their assigned locations. A **Viewer** has read-only access to their assigned locations. See [Users](/settings/users).

## Sentiment

Whether feedback is positive, neutral, or negative. Feedelity sets the sentiment for each tag on a case.

## Smart snippet

A fact or policy, such as "We don't take reservations", that your AI reply agents use in a reply when it's relevant. See [AI reply agents](/automation/agents#smart-snippets).

## Submission

One guest's answers to a form. Each submission arrives in your inbox as its own case. See [View submissions](/collecting-feedback/submissions).

## Tag

A label on a case that shows what the feedback is about, with a sentiment. Tags power your dashboards. See [Tags, sentiment, and priority](/inbox/tags-sentiment-priority).

## Task

A prioritized action Feedelity creates from recurring issues in your feedback. See [Tasks](/issues-and-tasks/tasks).

## Template

A saved reply you can insert in the inbox in one click. See [Response templates](/automation/templates).

## Topic and attribute

The parts of your business that opinions are about. A topic is the thing guests talk about, such as fries or staff. An attribute is the quality they mention, such as temperature or friendliness. Together they're also called aspects.

## Voucher

One guest's copy of an offer, with a unique code to redeem with you. Every voucher is listed under **Settings** > **Vouchers**. See [Vouchers](/loyalty/vouchers).

## Voucher code

The code on a voucher that the guest shows at checkout. Feedelity generates it, takes it from your own codes, or gets it from Leat. See [Voucher codes](/loyalty/feedelity-offers#voucher-codes).
