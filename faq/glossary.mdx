---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The terms used in Feedelity."
---

## AI reply agent

An AI assistant that drafts replies to your customers in your brand's tone. You set it up under **Settings** > **Agents**. See [AI reply agents](/automation/agents).

## Case

One piece of feedback in your inbox, such as a review, a message, or an email. A case holds the customer's message and all replies to it. See [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

## Channel

A feedback source for one location, such as a Google Business profile or an Uber Eats store. You connect channels through an integration and link each one to a location. See [How integrations work](/integrations/how-integrations-work).

## Folder

Your own category for cases, such as complaints or catering requests. Each case can be in one folder. See [Folders](/inbox/folders).

## Integration

The connection between Feedelity and an account on a platform, such as Google or Uber Eats. One integration can bring in many channels. See [Manage and reconnect integrations](/integrations/manage).

## Issue

A specific problem a customer ran into that you can fix, such as "Food served cold". Feedelity finds issues in your feedback automatically. See [Issues](/issues-and-tasks/issues).

## Location

A single restaurant. Each case belongs to a location, so you can filter the inbox and dashboards by restaurant. See [Locations](/settings/locations).

## Location group

A set of locations you group together, for example by region or brand. Use it to filter several locations at once. See [Location groups](/settings/location-groups).

## Message

One entry in a case. The customer's review or email is a message, and so is each reply your team sends.

## Opinion

One statement about one topic inside a piece of feedback. "The burger was great, but the fries were cold" holds two opinions. Each opinion has its own sentiment. Also called a claim. See [Opinions](/analytics/opinions).

## Organization

Your company's account in Feedelity, usually one restaurant brand. An organization holds your locations, users, and settings. You can belong to more than one organization.

## Red flag

An urgent issue in a piece of feedback, such as food poisoning. Feedelity alerts the teammates who have access to that location. See [Red flags](/settings/flags).

## Role

What a user can do in an organization. An **Administrator** has full access to all locations and settings. A **Member** can view, edit, and reply to feedback for their assigned locations. A **Viewer** has read-only access to their assigned locations. See [Users](/settings/users).

## Sentiment

Whether feedback is positive, neutral, or negative. Feedelity sets the sentiment for each tag on a case.

## Tag

A label on a case that shows what the feedback is about, with a sentiment. Tags power your dashboards. See [Tags, sentiment, and priority](/inbox/tags-sentiment-priority).

## Task

A prioritized action Feedelity creates from recurring issues in your feedback. See [Tasks](/issues-and-tasks/tasks).

## Template

A saved reply you can insert in the inbox in one click. See [Response templates](/automation/templates).

## Topic and attribute

The parts of your business that opinions are about. A topic is the thing guests talk about, such as fries or staff. An attribute is the quality they mention, such as temperature or friendliness. Together they're also called aspects.
