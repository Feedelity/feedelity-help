title: "License inactive"

---

Your organization needs an active Feedelity license to use the inbox, analytics, and the rest of the app. When the license is no longer active, you lose access until it's renewed.

This usually happens when a trial ends, a payment fails, or a subscription is canceled.

## What you see

What you see depends on your role:

- **Members and viewers** see the **License inactive** page: "Your organization's Feedelity license is no longer active."
- **Administrators** go straight to **Settings** > **Billing**, where they can renew the license.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /license-inactive — the License inactive page with the error banner */}

## Renew the license

If you're a member or viewer, ask an administrator of your organization to renew the license. You get your inbox and analytics back as soon as they do.

If you're an administrator, open **Settings** > **Billing**. A banner tells you what's wrong:

- **No active license. Choose a plan to continue using Feedelity.** Your trial or plan has ended. Pick a plan in the pricing table: click **Choose Standard**, or **Contact sales** for the Enterprise plan.
- **Your last payment didn't go through.** Click **Update payment details** and update your payment method.
- **Your subscription is paused.** Click **Update payment details** to resume it.

{/* SCREENSHOT: /settings/billing — the No active license banner above the pricing table */}

On a free trial, the banner **Your free trial ends on *date*** warns you before the trial runs out. Choose a plan before that date to keep access.

See [Billing and license](/settings/billing) for more about plans and invoices.

## While the license is inactive

- You regain full access to your data when the license is renewed.
- Daily and weekly summary emails stop.
- Red flags detected automatically don't send emails or push notifications.
