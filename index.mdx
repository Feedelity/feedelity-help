Feedelity is a guest feedback and review management platform for multi-location restaurant chains. It collects reviews and messages from all your channels in one inbox, drafts on-brand replies with AI, and turns feedback into dashboards, alerts, and tasks per location.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One inbox for all feedback" icon="inbox">
    Reviews and messages from Google, Tripadvisor, Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat Takeaway, Facebook, Instagram, and email arrive in a single inbox.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI replies in your tone" icon="sparkles">
    Your AI reply agent drafts replies in your brand's tone of voice. Approve each draft, or set up auto reply rules.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dashboards per location" icon="chart-column">
    Every case gets tags and a sentiment score. Compare ratings, topics, and complaints across locations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Red flags and tasks" icon="flag">
    Urgent issues, such as hygiene complaints, alert the right location manager. Tasks turn recurring feedback into a prioritized to-do list.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
