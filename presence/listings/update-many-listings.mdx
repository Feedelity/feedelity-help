---
title: "Update many listings at once"
description: "Keep the hours of all your locations up to date in one go, instead of one listing at a time."
---

Change the opening hours or add special hours on many listings in one go, for example when all your locations close for a holiday. Only administrators can update several listings at once.

## Select and update your listings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your listings">
    Go to **Online presence** > **Listings**. Tick the box in front of each listing. Tick the box in the header to select all listings that match your search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to change">
    In the bar at the bottom of the page, click **Edit opening hours** or **Add special hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the hours">
    Set the hours as you would for one listing, then click **Save**. See [Edit opening hours](/presence/listings#edit-opening-hours) and [Plan special hours](/presence/listings#plan-special-hours).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the update">
    Feedelity updates your listings one by one. Keep the window open until all listings are updated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Each listing shows **Updated**, **Failed**, or **Not supported**. **Not supported** means the platform of that listing doesn't allow this change. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the cross in the bar to clear your selection.

## Edit opening hours

**Edit opening hours** replaces the opening hours of every selected listing. If all selected listings have the same opening hours, the window starts from those hours. Otherwise, it starts from Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00.

## Add special hours

**Add special hours** adds the dates to every selected listing and keeps their other special hours. If a listing already has special hours on one of the dates, they're replaced.

To remove special hours, open each listing. See [Plan special hours](/presence/listings#plan-special-hours).
