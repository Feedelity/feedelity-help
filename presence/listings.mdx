The **Listings** page shows your listings on your connected platforms, such as your Google Business Profile listings. Update your details, categories, opening hours, special hours, and photos here, and Feedelity changes them on the platform right away. Open it from **Online presence** > **Listings** in the sidebar.

<Note>
  **Listings** isn't turned on for every organization. If you don't see **Online presence** > **Listings** in the sidebar, contact your account manager to turn it on.

  Some existing customers get access to try it out. They see a gem icon next to **Listings**, which marks it as part of **Feedelity Pro**.
</Note>

Today, you can manage your Google listings here. Only administrators can make changes. Members and viewers can see your listings, but not edit them.

To share news, events, and offers on your listings, see [Posts](/presence/posts).

## Your listings at a glance

The **Listings** page shows a table of your listings. Search for a listing by name or address with **Search listings...**.

- **Listing**: the name and address of the listing.
- **Opening hours**: whether the listing is open right now, for example **Open · Closes at 22:00** or **Closed · Opens at 09:00**. **Closes soon** and **Opens soon** show when the listing closes or opens within the hour. Special hours count for today too. If a listing is no longer connected, you see its connection status here instead.
- **Profile strength**: how complete the listing is, as a percentage. A green check means the listing is complete. See [Complete your listing](#complete-your-listing).

Click a listing to open it.

## Complete your listing

The **Complete your listing** card next to a listing shows how complete it is, as a percentage. Complete listings show up more often and win more visits.

A Google listing is complete when it has these 11 things:

- A cover photo
- A name
- Additional categories
- An address
- A phone number
- A website
- A description
- Opening hours, with at least one day open
- Special hours, with at least one upcoming date
- A logo
- Additional images

The card lists what's still missing. The next item is open, with a short explanation and a button to add it, such as **Add phone number** or **Add special hours**. Click another item to open it. Click **Show completed** to see what's already done.

When the listing is complete, the card disappears.

## Edit details

The **Details** card shows the **Name**, **Categories**, **Address**, **Phone number**, **Website**, and **Description** of the listing. A dot next to a field means it's missing.

1. Click the pencil next to a field. For an empty field, click its add button, such as **Add website**.
2. Change the value.
3. Click **Save**.

- **Name**: a listing always needs a name. Use the name customers know you by, without extra keywords.
- **Address**: once a listing has an address, you can change it but not remove it. You can't change the country.
- **Phone number**: choose the country, then type the number. Feedelity checks that the number is valid for that country before it saves.
- **Website**: you can leave out `https://`. Feedelity adds it for you.
- **Description**: up to 750 characters, without links or promotions.

Click the phone number or the website to copy it.

## Edit categories

Categories tell customers and the platform what your business is. The **Primary** category is the main one. Additional categories cover the other services you offer, so customers find you in more searches.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the categories">
    In the **Details** card, click the pencil next to **Categories**. If the listing has no categories yet, click **Add categories**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the primary category">
    Under **Primary category**, click **Set primary category** and search for the category that describes your business best. Choose one as specific as possible. To change it, click the cross on the current one first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add additional categories">
    Under **Additional categories**, click **Add category** and search for each one. Google allows up to 9 additional categories. Click the cross on a category to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit opening hours

1. In the **Opening hours** card, click **Edit**. If the listing has no opening hours yet, click **Add opening hours**.
2. For each day, turn the switch on to open or off to close.
3. Type or pick the opening and closing time. Click the plus to add another time slot on the same day, for example for a lunch break.
4. Click **Save**.

When you add opening hours for the first time, Feedelity fills in Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, so you only adjust what's different.

To give other days the same hours, click the copy icon next to a day. Choose the days and click **Copy opening hours**.

Time slots can't overlap, also not across days. For example, if Friday closes at 02:00, Saturday can't open before 02:00. A closing time before the opening time means the listing closes after midnight. If slots overlap, you see **Hours can't overlap.**

Today is highlighted in the **Opening hours** card.

## Plan special hours

Use special hours for holidays and other days with different hours. The **Special hours** card lists your upcoming dates by year, with the name of the public holiday when there is one.

To add special hours:

1. In the **Special hours** card, click **Add**. If there are no special hours yet, click **Add special hours**.
2. Click **Select a date or period**. Pick one day, or pick a first and a last day for a period, such as a holiday closure.
3. Leave the switch off to close for the whole day or period. Turn it on to set different hours.
4. To add more dates at once, click **Add more special hours**. Click the copy icon on a row to start a new row with the same hours.
5. Click **Save**.

You can't pick a date in the past, or a date that already has special hours.

To remove a date, click the three dots next to it and click **Delete**. Confirm with **Delete**. Customers then see your regular opening hours for that day again. To change the hours of a date, delete it and add it again.

## Manage photos

Photos show customers what to expect. The listing page has two places for them.

- **Cover photo**: the first image people see on your listing.
- **Media**: your logo and your other photos.

### Add a photo

1. Click **Add cover photo**, **Add logo**, or **Add photo**.
2. Drop a photo in the window, or click to browse.
3. Click **Save**.

Photos must be JPEG or PNG files of up to 5 MB. Photos you add under **Media** must also be at least 10 KB and at least 250 × 250 pixels. If a photo doesn't meet these rules, Feedelity tells you why before it uploads.

### Replace or delete a photo

- **Cover photo**: click the pencil on the cover photo. Then click **Replace cover photo** or **Delete cover photo**.
- **Logo**: click the logo. Then click **Replace logo** or **Delete logo**.
- **Other photos**: hover over a photo and click the bin to delete it.

Confirm with **Delete**. Customers no longer see the photo on the platform.

Click a photo under **Media** to see it full size. Use the arrows to go through your photos.

## When a platform rejects a change

The platform checks every change before it accepts it. If it rejects one, Feedelity shows the reason in the window, for example when a website link isn't accepted. Fix the value and click **Save** again.
