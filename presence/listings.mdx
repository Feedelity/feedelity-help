The **Listings** page shows your listings on your connected platforms, such as your Google Business Profile listings. Update your business information, opening hours, and special hours here, and Feedelity changes them on the platform right away. Open it from **Presence** > **Listings** in the sidebar.

<Note>
  **Listings** isn't turned on for every organization. If you don't see **Presence** > **Listings** in the sidebar, contact your account manager to turn it on.

  Some existing customers get access to try it out. They see a gem icon next to **Listings**, which marks it as part of **Feedelity Pro**.
</Note>

Today, you can manage your Google listings here. Only administrators can make changes. Members and viewers can see your listings, but not edit them.

## Your listings at a glance

The **Listings** page shows a table of your listings. Search for a listing by name or address with **Search listings...**.

- **Listing**: the name and address of the listing, and its connection status.
- **Opening hours**: whether the listing is open right now, for example **Open · Closes at 22:00** or **Closed · Opens at 09:00**. **Closes soon** and **Opens soon** show when the listing closes or opens within the hour. Special hours count for today too.
- **Profile**: how complete the listing is, for example **5 of 7**.

Click a listing to open it.

If you haven't connected a platform yet, you see **No listings yet**. Click **Go to integrations** to connect one. See [Google](/integrations/google).

## Complete your profile

The card at the top of a listing shows how complete the listing is, as a percentage. Complete listings show up more often and win more visits.

A listing is complete when it has these 7 things:

- A name
- A description
- An address
- A phone number
- A website
- Opening hours, with at least one day open
- Special hours, with at least one upcoming date

The card shows a button for each missing item, such as **Add phone number** or **Add special hours**. Click it to fill that item in right away.

To see the listing on the platform itself, click **View on Google** under the title.

## Edit business information

The **Business information** card shows the **Name**, **Description**, **Address**, **Phone number**, and **Website** of the listing.

1. Click the pencil next to a field. For an empty field, click its add button, such as **Add website**.
2. Change the value.
3. Click **Save**.

When the platform accepts the change, you see **Saved.**

- **Name**: a listing always needs a name.
- **Address**: once a listing has an address, you can change it but not remove it. You can't change the country.
- **Phone number**: choose the country, then type the number. Feedelity checks that the number is valid for that country before it saves.
- **Website**: you can leave out `https://`. Feedelity adds it for you.

Click the phone number or the website to copy it.

## Edit opening hours

1. In the **Opening hours** card, click **Edit**. If the listing has no opening hours yet, click **Add opening hours**.
2. For each day, turn the switch on to open or off to close.
3. Pick the opening and closing time. Click **Add hours** to add a second time slot on the same day, for example for a lunch break.
4. Click **Save**.

When you add opening hours for the first time, Feedelity fills in Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, so you only adjust what's different.

Time slots on the same day can't overlap, and the closing time must differ from the opening time. A closing time before the opening time means the listing closes after midnight.

Today is highlighted in the **Opening hours** card.

## Plan special hours

Use special hours for holidays and other days with different hours. The **Special hours** card lists your upcoming dates by year, with the name of the public holiday when there is one.

To add a date:

1. In the **Special hours** card, click **Add date**. If there are no special hours yet, click **Add special hours**.
2. Under **Date**, pick the day.
3. Under **Hours**, choose **Closed all day** or **Open with different hours**. For different hours, set the time slots.
4. Click **Save**.

To change or remove a date, click it in the **Special hours** card. Change the hours and click **Save**, or click **Delete**.

## When a platform rejects a change

The platform checks every change before it accepts it. If it rejects one, Feedelity shows the reason under the field, for example when a website link isn't accepted. Fix the value and click **Save** again.
