The **Posts** page shows the posts on your listings, such as the updates, events, and offers on your Google Business Profile. Write a post once and publish it to as many listings as you like. Open it from **Online presence** > **Posts** in the sidebar.

<Note>
  **Posts** isn't turned on for every organization. If you don't see **Online presence** > **Posts** in the sidebar, contact your account manager to turn it on.
</Note>

Today, you can post on your Google listings. Only administrators can create and delete posts. Members and viewers can see them.

## Your posts at a glance

Each card shows one post, with its photo, title, and date. **Posted** shows when it went live, and **Scheduled** shows when Google will publish it. A post you published to several listings at once shows as one card.

The badges on a card count the listings per status:

- **Live**: customers see the post on Google.
- **Scheduled**: Google publishes the post later.
- **Processing**: Google is still checking the post.
- **Rejected**: Google didn't accept the post.
- **Recurring**: the post repeats on Google.

Search for a post by its title or text with **Search posts**. Click a card to open the post.

## Create a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type of post">
    Click **New post** and choose the type:
    - **Update**: share news or an announcement.
    - **Event**: promote an upcoming event.
    - **Offer**: share a deal or promotion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the post">
    Fill in the fields, then click **Next**. See [What goes in a post](#what-goes-in-a-post).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your listings">
    On **Select your listings**, tick the listings to publish to, or tick **All listings**. Search to find a listing quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click the publish button, for example **Publish to 3 listings**. Feedelity publishes the post on each listing one by one. Keep the window open until it's done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Each listing shows the status of its post, or **Failed** if it couldn't be published. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What goes in a post

| Field | Update | Event | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title** | – | Required, up to 58 characters | Required, up to 58 characters |
| **Description** | Up to 1,500 characters | Optional, up to 1,500 characters | Optional, up to 1,500 characters |
| **Start date** and **End date** | – | Required, with an optional time | Required, with an optional time |
| **Photo** | One photo | One photo | One photo |
| **Button** | Optional | Optional | – |
| **Coupon code**, **Redeem link**, **Terms** | – | – | Optional |

An update needs a description or a photo.

Photos must be JPEG or PNG files between 10 KB and 5 MB, and at least 250 × 250 pixels.

To add a button, click **Button** and choose **Book**, **Buy**, **Call now**, **Learn more**, **Order online**, or **Sign up**. Add the link the button opens. **Call now** needs no link: it calls the phone number of the listing.

For an offer, click **Coupon code**, **Redeem link**, or **Terms** to add them. Click the cross next to a field to remove it.

## Check a post on each listing

Open a post to see it in full. The **Listings** list shows every listing it was published to, with its status on that listing. Search for a listing with **Search listings**. Click the arrow next to a listing to open the post on Google.

## Delete a post

- **From one listing**: click the bin next to the listing. Confirm with **Delete**. Your other listings keep the post.
- **From all listings**: click the three dots at the top of the page and click **Delete from all listings**. Confirm with **Delete from all listings**.

Feedelity removes the post from the listings on Google. You can't undo this.
